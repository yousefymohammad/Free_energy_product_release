# Inputs and parameter provenance

## Runtime inputs

The four GRO files in `inputs/coordinates/` are the original complete-system
starting structures, moved without changing their contents. They contain
protein coordinates, two ligands, solvent, counterions and velocities. They
represent the supplied previously simulated systems; they are not de novo
structures generated by this repository.

| System | Protein chains | Ligands | Choline | Chloride | Ethylene glycol | Water | Na ions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `chl_chcl_eg` | 2 | 2 CHL | 1266 | 1266 | 2532 | 16610 | 10 |
| `ctal_chcl_eg` | 2 | 2 CTAL | 1266 | 1266 | 2532 | 16610 | 10 |
| `chl_water` | 2 | 2 CHL | 0 | 0 | 0 | 19990 | 10 |
| `ctal_water` | 2 | 2 CTAL | 0 | 0 | 0 | 18990 | 10 |

Counts come from the supplied system topologies. The hydrated DES has a 1:2
ChCl:ethylene glycol ratio. `CHL` is naringenin chalcone; `CHO`, `CLO`, and `ETH`
are solvent molecule types. The topology charge sums are neutral to numerical
precision. Choline/chloride partial charges are scaled to +0.9/-0.9 per molecule
in the supplied DES parameterization.

The index files are copied unchanged from the original initial-coordinate
folder. `chaina.ndx` serves both DES systems because their atom ordering and
molecule sizes match. Its `chlb` group selects the second ligand in either case.
The misleading group label is retained to preserve existing index definitions.

`inputs/checksums.sha256` records the runtime GRO and NDX files and reference
PDBs. Check from the repository root with `sha256sum -c inputs/checksums.sha256`.

## Reference PDB files

`inputs/reference/` contains the original protein (`TaCHS.pdb`), CTAL
(`CTAL.pdb`), and component structures (`choline.pdb`, `chloride.pdb`, and
`Ethylene_glycol.pdb`). These files are supporting material and are not used by
the five runtime scripts. Several contain many molecules, rather than a single
isolated molecule. The CTAL reference uses the PDB residue label `CTA`.

The old file named `CHL.pdb` was byte-identical to `choline.pdb` and contained
choline atoms; it was removed as a misleading duplicate. A separate isolated
naringenin-chalcone reference PDB is not supplied. Its coordinates are present
in the CHL complete-system GRO files.

## Force field and molecule parameters

The supplied directory is named `amber14sb_parmbsc1.ff`. Its original
`forcefield.itp` still declares `_FF_AMBER12SB`; the directory name alone does
not establish the exact parameter version. The upstream `README.md`, header
comments, and numerical parameter files are retained. Confirm the intended
protein parameter provenance before reporting a particular Amber version in a
publication. Its README also describes a sodium Lennard–Jones correction.

- Protein chains use the supplied `topol_Protein_chain_A.itp` and `_B.itp`.
- CHL and CTAL files were generated by ACPYPE; their headers identify generation
  dates. Their atom types and numerical ligand parameters are preserved.
- `choline.itp` attributes its source to Perkins et al. and files supplied by
  Ferreira et al., with 0.9-scaled partial charges.
- `eth.itp` records modifications based on Perkins et al. (2014), including
  hydroxyl hydrogen and oxygen Lennard–Jones choices.
- `cl.itp` supplies the chloride molecule definition. The full systems include
  TIP3P water and sodium ion definitions from the supplied force field.

The current source does not identify every parameter's publication, original
upstream distribution version, or redistribution license. No blanket claim of
force-field validation or relicensing is made. Preserve upstream attribution
and establish the corresponding terms before public redistribution.

## Position-restraint scope

`-DPOSRES` in the system-level preparation NVT/NPT MDPs activates the includes
present in each molecule ITP. In the supplied files, this restrains protein
chains, CHL, choline and ethylene glycol when those molecule types are present.
The chloride restraint include is commented out. CTAL's ITP does not include its
available `posre_CTAL.itp`. Thus the two ligand types do not currently have the
same position-restraint policy during initial preparation. This inherited
policy is documented rather than silently changed.

Window equilibration and production use a harmonic pulling-coordinate restraint
and no `POSRES` define. All residue IDs, atom names, bond parameters, charges,
and index group membership are preserved during repository organization.
